## feed

You've landed on the [Atom feed](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4287.txt) of my notes for RSS readers. Every note ends up here. Some of them may puzzle you, since I write them first of all for myself.

If you meant to get to the site, it's [here](https://sanch.pet/). If you're curious how to add other people's feeds to a reader, have a look at [About Feeds](https://aboutfeeds.com/).

> [!note] How to subscribe
> In most slash-command interfaces it's enough to run `/feed subscribe https://sanch.pet/en/feed.xml`

## language

The site is bilingual. I think and write in Russian, but that may change sometime in the future. I try to duplicate the materials in two languages whenever possible.
